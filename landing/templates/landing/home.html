Ops Management System

A Practical Internal Operations System
for Businesses

Ops Management System is a lightweight internal platform to manage orders, inventory, staff tasks, and payments — built to replace spreadsheets, manual follow-ups, and fragmented workflows.

Explore Live Demo View Source Code

The problem this explores

Many small businesses still manage operations using Excel sheets, WhatsApp messages, and manual coordination.

This project explores what happens when internal operations are treated like a real product — with clear workflows, accountability, and real-time visibility.

📦 Orders & Inventory

Manage orders end-to-end, track inventory levels, record stock movements, and prevent inconsistencies using transactional safety.

✅ Task Automation

Tasks are auto-created from templates and assigned to staff, ensuring ownership and reducing manual coordination.

📊 Dashboards & Visibility

Separate dashboards for admins and staff provide real-time insight into orders, tasks, payments, and operational health.

How it works

1. Create Order

Orders are created and inventory is safely reserved.

2. Auto Tasks

Tasks are generated automatically from templates.

3. Staff Execution

Staff update task progress through a clean dashboard.

4. Completion & Payments

Orders complete automatically; payments are tracked or refunded.

Who this is for

  • • Grocery & retail stores
  • • Small distribution businesses
  • • Operations-heavy teams
  • • Founders exploring internal tools

This is a demo system — not a commercial SaaS product.

It is built to showcase real-world operational workflows, backend design decisions, and deployment practices using Django.
